Named but not linked means the model knows you and sends the click elsewhere. Linked but only on branded prompts means you have brand recognition, not category retrieval. Absent everywhere means the content and citation work comes before the dashboard. The 5-method guide explains what to do with each result.

What this tool does not do

  • It does not call ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google. You run the prompts yourself, logged out where possible.
  • It does not produce a “visibility score.” A single number hides the useful split between named, linked and absent.
  • It does not replace a paid tracker. If you need recurring runs across many prompts and engines, that is what an AI visibility tool is for; compare the plan limits before paying.
